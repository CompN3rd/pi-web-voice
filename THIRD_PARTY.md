It includes the complete license text for each reviewed package: Lit and its BSD-3-Clause components; Apache-2.0 Transformers.js; MIT ONNX Runtime JavaScript; and the MIT public Pi Web helpers; and the MIT Jinja 0.5.3 implementation embedded in Transformers (not the separately installed 0.5.10).
